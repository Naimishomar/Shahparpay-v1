Shahparpay app: BHIM-style mockups, light and dark

Static design mockups for approval. The layout follows the BHIM pattern: navy header, round coloured action icons in a 4-column grid, a raised scan button in the bottom bar, a big amount entry with keypad, and a green success receipt. Branding, logo and PIN screen are Shahparpay's own, not BHIM's or NPCI's.
