Retailer app · visual direction

Two Routes to a Payments App

The retailer app currently runs a monochrome near-black palette. Both directions below trade that for a chromatic brand block — the structure Paytm and PhonePe share — while keeping every screen, service and destination the app already has.

Same information architecture in both: the five bottom-nav destinations stay Home · AEPS · Services · Reports · Settlement, Account still lives behind the header avatar, and every service name is the one already in SERVICE_ITEMS. What changes is colour, surface and hierarchy — not navigation. Balances and transactions shown are the real shapes your API returns.

Direction A

Violet — the PhonePe lineage

One saturated violet carries the brand. It fills the header block, tints service icons, and does nothing else — every surface below is white on warm grey. Money stays black; only status is coloured. This reads calm and reduces the chance a retailer misreads a failed transaction as a decorative accent.

Where it works

One hue means status colour never competes with brand colour — the thing that matters most on a money screen.

Where it strains

Violet at full saturation across a tall header can feel heavy on small budget screens in daylight.

Direction B

Navy & cyan — the Paytm lineage

A two-tone system: deep navy carries authority in the header, cyan carries action on buttons and the active nav state. Balance sits on a raised white card that overlaps the header, so the number a retailer checks fifty times a day is the brightest object on the screen.

Where it works

Cyan gives you a distinct action colour, so “Add money” never looks like a heading. The overlapping balance card is the strongest hierarchy of the two.

Where it strains

Cyan on white fails contrast for text — it can only ever be a background with navy text on it, never a text colour.

What both directions fix

Balance A row among other rows Owns the header block or an overlapping card It is the single most-checked number in the app
Status Colour on text Filled pill, colour + word Colour alone fails colour-blind retailers and daylight glare
Amounts Proportional figures tabular-nums everywhere Columns of money stop jittering between rows
Blocked actions Greyed button, no reason Button states the blocker “Capture fingerprint to continue” beats a dead grey button
Report summary Below the list Four tiles above the list Summary before detail is how a scanned screen should read

Both directions hold the constraints the app already respects: five bottom-nav destinations with icon and label, 44pt minimum touch targets, 4.5:1 body-text contrast, and an 8px spacing rhythm. Nothing here requires a navigation change or a new screen — it is a token swap plus a re-layout of Home and Reports.
